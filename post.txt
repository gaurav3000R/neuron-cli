🚀 Want an AI assistant that lives in your Terminal, your Browser, AND your Slack? Meet Neuron. 🧠

I’m building Neuron—a local-first, Go-powered AI CLI that gives you total flexibility in how you work. It’s still under development, but the core "Access Anywhere" vision is already alive!

Whether you're deep in the code or away from your desk, Neuron has an interface for you:

📟 THE INTERFACE OPTIONS:
✅ TUI (Terminal UI): A beautiful, animated terminal interface for the keyboard-warriors who never want to leave the CLI.
✅ Web UI: A sleek, dark-mode React app embedded directly into the binary. Just run `neuron ui` and it opens in your browser.
✅ Slack Bot: Integrated Socket Mode support. Chat with your local LLMs directly from Slack for seamless remote access.

🛠️ CORE FEATURES:
• Multi-Provider: Switch between local (Ollama) and cloud (OpenAI, Gemini).
• Agentic Tools: It can read/write files and run shell commands (with your approval!).
• Private & Secure: Your data stays local by default.
• Code-Aware: Automatically ingests your project's .cursorrules and READMEs.

🚧 THE FUTURE ROADMAP:
• 🛠️ Docker Sandboxing: Moving tool execution to isolated containers.
• 📂 Persistent Sessions: SQLite-backed history for all your chats.
• 📱 WhatsApp Access: Bringing remote AI assistance to your favorite messaging app.
• 🔍 Deep Context (RAG): Advanced search for massive codebases.

Neuron isn't just a chatbot; it's a portable, multi-interface brain for your development workflow.
